A Short November Walk at Riverstone Park

By the Everbowl Spokane kitchen · November 22, 2026 · 3 min read

Riverstone Park in Coeur d’Alene is a good North Idaho outing when you have part of an afternoon and people who want different things from it. One person can go for a walk, someone else can sit for a few minutes, and kids can make the playground the main event. The City of Coeur d’Alene’s park page lists paths for pedestrians and cyclists, benches, a playground, and off-street parking at 1805 Tilford Lane.

Use the amphitheater as your turnaround

Start near the parking area and follow an open park path toward the amphitheater. You can turn back there for a short outing or keep going along the park paths if everyone is still enjoying the walk. Give cyclists room to pass, and use one of the benches for a break instead of stopping across the path. If you’re meeting someone after the first part of the walk, the amphitheater is an easy landmark to agree on before you arrive.

On a November visit, I’d start with the walk and save the playground for the return. That lets you see how the group feels before committing to a longer stretch away from the car. If there are wet leaves or frost underfoot, choose the clearer path and keep the outing short. Bring a layer you can put back on when you stop moving.

Check the seasonal details

The city’s parks FAQ lists city park hours of 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. and says park restrooms generally close in mid-October. For a late-fall visit, plan to arrive in daylight and make a restroom stop beforehand. The city also lists Riverstone’s paths among the places dogs can join a walk on leash.
