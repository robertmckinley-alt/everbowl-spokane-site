A Short Fish Lake Trail Walk and PB Cacao Dream

By the Everbowl Spokane kitchen · November 17, 2026 · 3 min read

The Fish Lake Trail is a good choice when you want a bit of Inland Northwest scenery and an outing you can shorten on the fly. This paved rail trail starts in West Spokane near Government Way and Sunset Boulevard. An out-and-back gives you an easy way to choose the distance once you’re there, whether you’re walking with a friend or taking the family out for some fresh air between weekend plans.

Pick a time to turn around

Use the Washington Trails Association guide to find the start and get oriented. Set a time to turn around before you leave the trailhead. For a 40-minute outing, walk about 20 minutes out and allow the rest for the return. If the group wants to stop for photos or take a longer break, begin the return a little earlier. The old rail grade makes the route feel straightforward and gives you a good sense of your pace without a complicated itinerary.

Remember that Fish Lake Trail is a shared route. Leave room for people passing on bikes, and step clear of the main path when you want to stop and talk. In late fall, look out for wet leaves or slick sections in the shade, and shorten the walk if the surface is uncomfortable for anyone in the group.

Use the West Spokane trailhead as your landmark

The name can suggest the paved route takes you straight to Fish Lake. The city’s trail update identifies a remaining gap. For this plan, use the West Spokane trailhead, make it an out-and-back, and skip the logistics of arranging a second car. If you want to explore farther on another day, check the current route information before you set an endpoint.

Choose a smoothie for the way back

If the rest of your day takes you east, Everbowl Spokane Valley at 13324 E. Sprague Ave., Suite 101 offers two different directions. PB Cacao Dream combines Cacao Wow, banana, cacao nibs, peanut butter, and almond milk. Evergreen blends Blue Majic, Coco Love, mango, pineapple, spinach, and apple juice. Choose PB Cacao Dream for the peanut butter and cacao pairing, or Evergreen when you’re in the mood for pineapple and mango.

If you’re making plans with friends, send them both the trail guide and the Spokane Valley store details so they know where to meet and can check the latest opening information.
