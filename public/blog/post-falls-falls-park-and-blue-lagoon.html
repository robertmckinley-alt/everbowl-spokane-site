Falls Park in Post Falls and a Blue Lagoon Bowl

By the Everbowl Spokane kitchen · November 17, 2026 · 3 min read

Falls Park makes a good first stop on a North Idaho day when you want to see the Spokane River and still leave most of the day open. At 305 W. 4th Ave. in Post Falls, the city park has paved paths, signs that explain the area’s history, a playground, and views of the dam and gorge. You can make the overlook the whole outing or add time at the playground if you’re traveling with kids.

Start with the gorge

Walk to an open viewpoint and give yourself time to look both upstream and downstream. The river and the structures beside it tell two parts of the same story. The city’s page on the dam gives a little background on the power plant and the mining communities it was built to serve. Read it before you go, or use the park’s signs to follow the story while you’re there.

If you’re showing a visitor around, the overlook is a good place to explain how close Spokane Valley and North Idaho are. The same river runs through both. Pay attention to how different the river looks here from the familiar views around Spokane. You can build a future half-day around another river stop and give each visit its own pace.

Keep the route easy to shorten

For a brief visit, pick one paved path and turn back when you’ve had your time at the viewpoint. If the group wants more, use the playground or the covered shelters to break up the outing. Bring an extra layer for a November visit so you can spend more than a minute at the overlook when the air cools off.

Before leaving, check the city’s park projects page for updates, especially if the pond is part of your plan. A route built around the overlook can still be worthwhile if work changes access to another part of the park.

Two bowls for the trip back west

If you’re headed back toward Spokane, look up Everbowl Spokane Valley at 13324 E. Sprague Ave., Suite 101. The Blue Lagoon layers Blue Majic, pitaya, Coco Love, and chia pudding with toppings that include strawberry and pineapple. The Berry Boost is a good pick if you’d rather have açaí, strawberry, blueberry, and goji berry with your chia pudding.

Pick the one with the fruit you want, or order both for the table if you’re out with family. View the current Spokane Valley store details before you drive over.
