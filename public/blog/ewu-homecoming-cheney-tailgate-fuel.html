EWU Homecoming Weekend: Fueling a Cheney Tailgate Without the Stadium Food Trap

By the Everbowl Spokane kitchen · October 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Every October, the parking lots around Roos Field in Cheney fill up hours before kickoff for Eastern Washington University's Homecoming game — grills going, red turf waiting, alumni back in town from all over the Inland Northwest. It's a genuinely fun Spokane Valley tradition, whether you're a current EWU family, an alum making the short drive out Highway 904, or just someone who likes a reason to tailgate on a crisp fall Saturday. The catch is the same one every tailgate has: between the early start, the standing around in cool air, and the chips-and-soda spread that shows up on every tailgate table, it's easy to end the day feeling worse than when you woke up.

Why tailgate days hit differently

A Homecoming Saturday asks more of your body than a normal weekend day. You're up earlier than usual to beat the parking crunch, standing on asphalt or grass for hours before the game even starts, and the temperature in Cheney by late October can swing 20-plus degrees between a chilly morning setup and an afternoon kickoff. Add in grazing on whatever's within reach at the tailgate — usually salty snacks and sugary drinks — and it's a recipe for an energy crash right around the third quarter, long before the game is decided.

A better pre-game plan

  • Eat a real breakfast before you load the car — not just coffee. Something with protein and fiber holds up far better than a donut from the gas station on the way to Cheney.
  • Hydrate before the grill fires up — tailgates run on beer and soda; building in water or a fruit-forward drink early keeps you from starting the day already behind.
  • Pack something that travels — a cooler full of ice doesn't keep a smoothie bowl intact, but a to-go cup or a toast wrapped in foil survives the drive out just fine.
  • Plan for halftime, not just pre-game — a second round of real food around the midpoint of the day keeps energy steadier than coasting on morning coffee alone.
The tailgates that are still having fun in the fourth quarter are usually the ones who ate something real before the first whistle, not just the ones who got there earliest.

Order ahead, then head out Highway 904

Everbowl Spokane sits at 13324 E Sprague Ave, Suite 101 in Spokane Valley, which makes it an easy stop on the way out to Cheney for an early kickoff. A Bruschetta Avocado Toast is substantial enough to actually hold you through pre-game setup, pairing fiber and healthy fat in a way that a bag of chips never will. For something to sip on the drive, an Ice Blended Matcha brings steady, jitter-free caffeine instead of the sugar spike of a gas-station energy drink — a real advantage when you're standing in a parking lot for three hours before kickoff. If the group is splitting up a cooler run, order ahead online so your food is ready when you swing by, instead of adding a wait to an already early morning.

For halftime or the drive home, a Dragon Fruit Lemonade is a genuinely refreshing reset after a few hours in the sun or the cold — real fruit instead of another can from the cooler, and bright enough to feel like a treat without the crash that follows a second soda.

Win or lose, refuel before the drive back

Whether EWU pulls off the Homecoming win or the day ends in a tougher loss, hours on your feet in variable fall weather is still a real physical day. Stopping back through the Valley on the way home for something with actual protein and carbs beats grabbing fast food on the way to I-90 — your body will thank you on Sunday morning either way.

The bottom line

Homecoming weekend in Cheney is one of the best reasons to tailgate in the Inland Northwest all fall, and it's easy to fuel it right without losing any of the fun. A real breakfast before you leave the Valley, a plan for halftime, and a stop at Everbowl on either end of the drive keep the day feeling good well after the final whistle.
