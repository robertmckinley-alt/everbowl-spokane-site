A Manito Park Walk and Classic Avocado Toast

By the Everbowl Spokane kitchen · November 17, 2026 · 3 min read

Manito Park is an easy outing to fit into a short day on Spokane’s South Hill. Give yourself time to look around Duncan Garden, see what’s on display at Gaiser Conservatory if it’s open, and take the rest of the park at whatever pace suits your group. This version works as a quick stop between errands or a longer walk with someone you haven’t caught up with in a while.

Start with Duncan Garden

The city describes Duncan Garden as a formal European Renaissance-style garden. In late fall, you can see its symmetrical layout especially clearly. Start with a look down the main axis and then walk around the garden. It’s a simple way to make the outing feel like you actually saw something, even if everyone is ready to go after one stop.

Give each person one thing to look for. A kid might count matching features on either side of the garden, while someone with a camera can look for a view that shows the pattern from ground level. If the light is flat, concentrate on details like the lines of the paths and the shapes of the garden beds.

Make the conservatory your second stop

Keep the Gaiser Conservatory page handy. If it’s open, the conservatory gives your outing a little variety after the outdoor walk. Pick a room or display to spend time in and compare what’s growing there with what you saw outside. If it’s closed, use the Manito Park guide to choose another part of the park for a turnaround.

For a family outing, plan around the stop the youngest person is most likely to enjoy. For a walking meetup, pick a clear meeting point at the park and let the route come together from there. The advantage of Manito is that you can do a short visit without having to race across town for a second activity.

Pick a savory stop in Spokane Valley

On a day you’re heading east after the park, Everbowl Spokane Valley at 13324 E. Sprague Ave., Suite 101 is a stop to keep in mind. The Classic Avocado Toast comes with avocado spread, everything seasoning, chili flakes, and honey. The Bruschetta Avocado Toast puts tomatoes, basil, and balsamic glaze in the spotlight. If two people are splitting lunch, ordering one of each is a direct way to try the two styles side by side.

Looking for a drink to go with it? Iced Matcha is on the drinks menu, alongside the lemonades and cold brews. Check the Spokane Valley location page for the latest store details before making the stop.
